The full subtitle layout/draw scaling design and signatures are adapted from
DSOpt by Luminous:
https://github.com/Kanadeforever/DSOpt
Used under the MIT License.

It also redistributes SDL3.dll and embeds MinHook through that project.
The generated game font atlases use NanumBarunGothic Regular, Copyright (c)
2010 NAVER Corporation, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The Windows installer is built with NSIS and uses the bundled VPatch plug-in
to generate patched STR files from a user's supported original game files.

The Linux AppImage GUI is based in part on the work of the FLTK project
(https://www.fltk.org) through fltk-rs. It embeds the AppImage type-2 runtime.
Applicable notices and the locked Rust dependency inventory are included in
third_party/licenses and linux-installer/RUST_DEPENDENCIES.md.
